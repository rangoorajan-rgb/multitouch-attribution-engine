# Multi-Touch Attribution Engine

## Business Challenge

FlowCRM is a fictional B2B SaaS company that invests in multiple marketing channels including Google Ads, LinkedIn Ads, Organic Search, Email Marketing and Direct Traffic.

The executive team currently relies on last-click attribution, making it difficult to understand how each marketing channel contributes throughout the customer journey.

Marketing believes upper-funnel channels such as SEO and LinkedIn are undervalued, while Finance questions the return on marketing investment.

This project demonstrates how different attribution models can produce different commercial insights using SQL, BigQuery and Looker Studio.

## Objectives

- Build a SQL-first attribution engine
- Demonstrate First-Touch, Last-Touch, Linear and Time-Decay attribution
- Compare attribution models side by side
- Create executive dashboards in Looker Studio
- Show how attribution influences budget allocation decisions
